# The ten directions

Decided on 2026-10-07 after a research round, five independent designer passes
(30 proposals), a curated slate, three adversarial critics and a final creative-director
call. Every direction follows `CONTRACT.md` and the binding rules at the end of this file.

| # | Direction | Category | The object | Where the 84 lives | Founder 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

territory the Codex exploration claimed. Écharpe sits near Codex's Terrace and Panneau near
its Broadcast; both are structurally different objects (a knitted record and a physical
paddle board).

## How the ten were chosen

1. **Research.** Five readers covered the brand system, the product surfaces where a card
   would live, a critique of the Codex exploration, Moroccan football youth culture (with
   sources) and collectible/status systems.
2. **Ideation.** Five designer lenses each proposed six directions: game UI, sports product
   and broadcast, collectible and brand identity, interaction and motion, and a Gen-Z
   designer from Casablanca. That gave 30 proposals.
3. **Impeccable direction roll** (`concept-seed --scope direction --mode experience`, key
   `1eb4cb75`). The grounded candidates in order of resonance were Écharpe, Panneau,
   Lucarne, Lsaq, Flocage, Tir and Pylône. The roll assigned index 3, **Lucarne**, which is
   built as direction 01. The six catalog challengers were fused and weighed on audience
   identification and product clarity:

   | Challenger | Verdict | What it donated |
   |---|---|---|
   | Bebop title card | declined | Freeze on impact (Lucarne): a hard cut and a one-beat freeze when the ball hits the corner |
   | Punk paste-up | declined | Generations (Pochoir): each season adds a coat of overspray around the cuts |
   | Akari light | declined | Light is the state (Quatre Ombres): the tier is the quality of the light, never a colour |
   | Łowicz papercut | competitive | Layer per tier, depth by overlap only |
   | CRT arcade | declined | Integer mesh (Lucarne, Écharpe): the net and the stitch grid snap to whole pixels at small sizes |
   | Teletext | declined | Hold, not rotate: the board pins the stat being read |

4. **Curation and stress test.** A creative-director agent curated a slate of ten. Three
   adversarial critics attacked it: clone and resemblance, small size / right-to-left /
   truth, and belonging, from the point of view of a 16-year-old from Casablanca and a
   38-year-old Fantasy veteran. The slate was then revised.
5. **Final call (Claude as creative director).**
   - **Ardoise** (the café chalk A-frame) was cut. All three critics rejected it: it reads
     as a menu or a price board; "ardoise" is a child's school slate and, in "avoir une
     ardoise", a debt; and it repeats the app's own "standings soon" easel.
     **Brassard** replaces it.
   - **Flocage** (the bench jacket with the number heat-pressed on its back) was cut on a
     product fact. At 24px it reads as the shirt the Fantasy squad already uses for players,
     and Pépites already puts a rank on a shirt back. **Porte-clés** replaces it.
   - **Pavé** (the paving block) was cut. The curator wanted to keep it, rebuilt as
     "Autobloquant", but a 16-year-old's identity as "the floor people walk on" is the
     weakest reason to belong in the pool, and a paving block is a riot projectile in
     French. **Semelle** replaces it, against the curator: its stud-pattern tier ladder is
     the strongest black-and-white ladder in the pool, and the brief prefers risks that
     fail over safe cards. Its honest weakness is a wide 24px mark.
   - The three cut directions were already half built when the critics reported. They are
     kept in the gallery's appendix with these reasons, as evidence of what was tried.

## Binding rules (after the critics)

These override every spec.

1. **HOMA is raw but new, never poor,** and it keeps the full silhouette of its direction.
2. **LEGEND changes the black-and-white outline** in one way visible at 32px, and keeps the
   stats on the front.
3. **Gold is rationed.** Precious metal appears at most where a direction names it as its one
   precious moment. Never black with brass or champagne.
4. **Founder 2026 is visible at arm's length** and leaves a sign of at least 3px at 24px. The
   preferred written form puts the year after the name, the way Moroccan supporter groups
   carry their founding year: **ALI ·26**.
5. **No ritual holds the number back.** The card always shows the current 84; a ceremony is
   an optional replay. Avoid anything that could be taken for a casino instant game, a
   lottery ball, a scratch card or an odds screen, and the words "pull", "pack" or "level up".
6. **OVR is never a tiny label stacked under the 84.** It is either inline as a unit or
   absent from the artwork.
7. **A ranking row only gets a mini of 24–28px inside its name cell,** built from the
   object's own parts. The 44–80px token belongs in the "My position" card, the hub and
   head-to-head.
8. **The chosen club's colour shows on the 44px token.**
9. **The logo is used unmodified:** only the colour and light files.
10. **The shared avatar has its hood up,** and is never shown as a centred solid bust.
11. **Names are set in Changa 800 in both scripts.**
12. **All labels come from the kit's strings,** and no sample data is invented.
13. **No turbulence or displacement filters in tokens and rows.**
14. **The ID and the season get a physical carrier native to the object.**
15. **Navy is not the default:** each object's material sets its colour.

## Open questions for the owner

- Is OVR capped at 99? Every two-digit slot has a 3-digit fallback at 82%, but the
  direction would be cleaner with a cap.
- Should the Latin card speak French (MAROC, FONDATEUR) rather than the sample's English
  (MOROCCO, FOUNDER)? The lab keeps the owner's sample strings so the comparison with Codex
  stays exact.
- Should Arabic tier names be translated (حومة, ملعب, محترف, بطل, أسطورة) or kept as Latin
  brand words? The lab translates them.
- For SEL in Arabic, the lab uses التشكيلة, because الاختيار already means "selected by X%" in
  the app. It needs a Modern Standard Arabic review.
- Tier logic, XP and OVR calculations are deliberately absent.
