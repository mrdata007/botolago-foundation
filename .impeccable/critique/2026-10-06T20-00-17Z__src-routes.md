## Overall Impression

A careful, identity-rich football product whose weakest layer is honesty about what is switched on, and whose main conversion (Fantasy) states wrong rules and hides the captain decision. Biggest opportunity: one switch-aware truth pass across every promise, then a clear captain/rules moment in Fantasy.

## What's Working

- Match page and standings: score-first stadium type over the club split; real table with zone bars, legend, provisoire, tie note, W/D/L fold, full Arabic mirroring.
- Data honesty where designed: "Le match n'a pas encore commencé", blank unknown shirt numbers, Méthode coverage table ("Pied fort connu 0 %"), prizes "100 % gratuit — aucun achat, aucun pari", winners empty state.
- Fantasy picker states every refusal before the tap ("Limite du club atteinte", "Au-dessus du budget", "Masquer les bloqués").
- Dark mode and RTL held on every screen checked; Pépites restyle complete.
