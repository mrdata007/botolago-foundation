# SEO pass 1 (27 October 2026)

Source: the owner's SEO brief of 27 October 2026 (Search Console export plus
checks of the live HTML). This pass takes the small, high-impact items of its
priority list. The larger ones are listed at the end, with what each needs.

The brief asks for kick-off times with a `+01:00` offset. Morocco keeps UTC+0
all year from 20 September 2026 (`src/lib/morocco-time.ts`), so times stay in
UTC (`Z`), which is exact.

## Screen change: the standings heading

The one visible change in this pass.

- **Preserved:** the title band, the season picker beside the title on a
  phone, the tabs, the table, every rule of the standings.
- **Improved:** the page's H1 names the page ("Classement" / "الترتيب")
  instead of the section ("Matchs"). "Classement Botola Pro" was tried first
  and pushed the season picker onto a second line at 390 px, so the page
  jumped between the Calendrier and Classement tabs; the full phrase stays in
  the `<title>` tag.
- **Acceptance:** at 390 px and 1280 px, in French and in Arabic (RTL), the
  heading and the season picker sit on one line, and the page has no
  horizontal overflow (measured: `scrollWidth` equals the viewport width in
  all four, and the heading is 184 px FR / 102 px AR wide).
