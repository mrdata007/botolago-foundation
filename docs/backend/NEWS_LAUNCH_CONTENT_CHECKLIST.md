# News launch content — checklist and template

News goes public only with **3 to 5 original, human-approved BotolaGO
stories**. Nothing here is to be invented: every fact needs a source a
reviewer has checked. Fill one copy of the template per story, get it signed
off, then enter it in the CMS (`/admin/news`).

## Before a story is entered

- [ ] Original BotolaGO writing. No copied paragraphs from another outlet.
- [ ] Every factual claim (score, injury, transfer, quote, date, availability)
      has a named source the reviewer has opened. Unconfirmed = not published.
- [ ] Quotes are exact and attributed to a named person, with where/when said.
- [ ] Cover photo: BotolaGO-owned, commissioned, or licensed with written
      permission. Record the licence. No screenshots of other sites, no
      agency photos without a licence, no images pulled from social media.
- [ ] One story chosen as **lead** (placement "Une des actualités" / "Une de
      l'accueil" after publication).
- [ ] Human approval recorded: reviewer name and date in the template.

## Entering it

1. Editor: *Nouvel article* → French edition → save.
2. Cover: fill alt, caption, credit, then choose the file. Save.
3. *Créer l'édition arabe* → Arabic edition (it is linked to the French one,
   so search engines and readers can switch between them). Save.
4. Editor sends each edition to review (*En relecture*).
5. Publisher checks it against this template, then *Publié* or *Programmé*.
6. Publisher sets the lead placement on the lead story.

## Not allowed

- Third-party full text, even with a link back.
- Photos without a licence, or hotlinked from another site (the public page
  removes them anyway).
- Placeholder or test text ("QA —" articles are for testing only and must
  stay unpublished).
- Republishing the archived imported stubs (they need an explicit conversion
  by an editorial admin, with a recorded reason, first).
