# BotolaGO editorial identity

Write for a supporter following the Botola, or a manager making a decision before a deadline. Lead with the score, state, consequence or action.

- French uses **vous**. Arabic addresses the reader in the singular, using clear football-media Arabic. Keep BotolaGO in its established Latin form and Prospects / المواهب as the section names.
- Live football: short states, actual match data, precise publication/confirmation status. An unconfirmed lineup is not a probable XI. Do not suggest that missing information confirms availability.
- Fantasy: explain the next action, the rule that constrains it, and whether changes are saved. Distinguish points from ratings, transfers from substitutions, point deductions from payments, and provisional from final results.
- News: factual headlines and source attribution. No promotional introductions or public AI authorship notices.
- Prospects: preserve age, competition, minutes, comparison cohort and statistical units. Distinguish score-based ranking from editorial selection.
- Manager Cards: explain sporting merit and meaningful thresholds; avoid repeating the rating/tier already visible. Styles do not affect ratings. Forming cards still need their missing-data explanation.
- Plus: use the established Saison vocabulary. Preserve price, end date, one-payment terms, manual receipt/verification and expiry consequences. Avoid permanent promises beyond the purchased access.
- Settings, security and errors: name the consequence and available recovery. Keep device/account language and storage distinctions. Staff screens can retain operational identifiers where staff actually need them.
- A subtitle must add a rule, useful scope, status or action. Remove it when the title and adjacent controls already provide its meaning; remove its wrapper/margin too.
- Keep useful uncertainty. Do not replace a missing-data state with zero, a network diagnosis, an invented launch date or a delivery guarantee.
- Translate meaning and football terminology rather than French syntax. Preserve placeholders, plural families and existing bidi isolation. Prefer Arabic “{n} من 4” to a mixed-direction fraction in prose.
- Keep the design system’s type sizes, Arabic leading, contrast tokens, logical alignment and control targets. Shorten text before considering layout changes; never hide necessary instructions with truncation.
