# BotolaGO UX Writing Specialist — independent source audit

**Evidence:** source inspection only. I did not run the application or claim browser, desktop, mobile, Arabic wrapping or screenshots were checked. Screen/visual agents own that evidence. No repository or database edits were made. Only files under /tmp were written.

Read AGENTS.md, CLAUDE.md, PRODUCT.md and the full user request first. Main dictionary keys are in `src/i18n/dictionary-fr.ts` and `src/i18n/dictionary-ar.ts`; dynamic card/Curva readers use `src/components/manager-card/copy.ts`, and Plus uses `src/components/plus/plus-copy.ts`.

## Coverage and disposition

Indexed all **3,258 French + 3,258 Arabic keys**. **2,403 keys** have direct literal references in public route/component TSX (plus dynamically selected keys). **621 supporting-text candidates** appear in the companion sentence inventory. Proposed **131 individual changes**: 17 REMOVE, 27 REWRITE, 80 SHORTEN, 7 REPOSITION. All other entries remain KEEP; this does not imply visual review of every variant. Exact originals, dictionary line numbers, references, FR/AR replacements and reasons are in the JSON.

## Highest-impact findings

1. **Payment instructions currently overpromise:** `PlusPayScreen.tsx` opens wa.me with message/code only. The receipt must be attached by the user; current `plus.pay.wa_hint` implies it goes automatically.
2. **Card style disclaimer is factually misleading:** `plus.rule` says the rating never changes. It means buying a style does not affect the rating.
3. **Sign-up and pronostics contain invented speed promises:** remove “moins d’une minute” and “en 1 minute”.
4. **Follow-player sign-up oversells unrelated actions:** following does not subscribe a person to weekly mail or add them to a Fantasy team.
5. **Many subtitles simply restate their headings:** notifications, saved news, rankings, Top 5, setup, callback, legal profile links, FAQ and final landing pitch.
6. **Data honesty needs plain language, not defensive prose:** remove “Nous n’en inventons pas”, cloud/mapping wording and invented imminent availability promises. Preserve uncertainty, correction windows, 5–16-day publication timing, and limitations that change a decision.
7. **Public-vs-private details are essential:** retain public display-name hints, recap publication privacy, trophy sharing scope, account-deletion deadlines and payment/entitlement expiry details.
