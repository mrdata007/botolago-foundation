# Manager Card collectible redesign: evidence index

Every acceptance criterion of the [brief](../MANAGER_CARD_SORARE_STYLE_BRIEF.md), the command that measured it, the
port, the number, and the file that holds it. The design is the [plan](../MANAGER_CARD_SORARE_STYLE_PLAN.md) (revision 3
sections win over older text) and the preview [`mock.html`](mock.html). The card is the collectible (Éclat, renderer
`eclat-v1`); the incumbent it replaces is the Écharpe on `main` at `8fae526c`.

Everything below was measured on `claude/manager-card-sorare-style` at **`5e903f60`** (the last source commit, after the
review fixes of plan §17), in `/home/user/mc-sorare`, with `src/`, `public/`, `scripts/` and `tests/` exactly as committed.
Nothing here touched a database, a migration, an Edge Function, a deployment or Lovable: every run used the mock data
modes (`VITE_*_DATA_MODE=mock`, `VITE_AUTH_MODE=mock`), or the production build against the e2e stub backend. Nothing was
pushed. One writer at a time: no other agent's server was running on this machine; the only servers were the ones named below, each started and stopped by its process id.

Measured as CLAUDE.md "Evidence" asks: overflow from element rectangles (never `scrollWidth`), contrast from rasterised
pixels (never from the colours in the markup), each run against a server started from the tree being measured on its own
port. Where a check did not give what the brief wants, the row says so, in numbers.

## What did not run, and why

- **A production figure for G1's time to card.** `MANAGER_CARD_PREVIEW` needs `import.meta.env.DEV`, so the section can only be
  drawn on a development server (unbundled modules, the development build of React). The figures below are upper bounds.
- **The tilt on a GPU.** The only browser here is headless Chromium with a software rasteriser. See finding 3.
- **Anything against a real backend or a real account.** Mock modes and fixtures only; no database access of any kind.
- **A real phone, Safari, a screen reader.** Not available. Accessible names are asserted by the render tests; contrast, overflow and
  touch sizes are measured in Chromium at phone widths.
- **A draft pull request.** This package may not push, so none was opened. [`PR_DESCRIPTION.md`](PR_DESCRIPTION.md) is the
  description, ready to paste, with the brief in it. Not merged, not published, nothing deployed.
- **The Impeccable detector on the `.ts` files** that draw the card's SVG: it reads `.tsx` and `.css` only (see criterion F9).
- **The after pictures with the section off.** With the section off the app is today's app; that is shown by the switch-off
  comparison (pictures of the production builds), not by another picture set.

## Findings that need the owner's eye

These came out of this evidence run. None was changed here (this package measures, it does not edit the app).

1. **G1 in French, 768 px and wider: « Les vôtres » is cut to « Les … ».** The card's column is now 352 px (was 264), so the
   people block's column is narrower: the heading's box is 63 px and its text needs 103. Arabic is not affected. Before: the
   heading is whole. Measured with `heading.scrollWidth > clientWidth` at 768, 1024, 1280 and 1440 (French: truncated at all
   four, Arabic: at none); picture: [`compare/g1-rated-light-1440.webp`](compare/g1-rated-light-1440.webp), French after; [`results/heading-truncation.txt`](wp4/results/heading-truncation.txt). The
   repository's layout probe does not flag it (it keeps 61 % of the word, its threshold is 60 %).
2. **G1's first screen on a phone.** At 390 × 844 the next-round line now ends 7.55 px (French) and 2.61 px (Arabic) above the
   bottom bar for every card state (it ended 38 to 152 px above in French, and 22 px under to 113 px above in Arabic, with the
   scarf). At 360 × 740 it ends 96 px (French) and 101 px (Arabic) **under** the bar for every state, and the identity line (« Depuis la J5 · Raja … ») 52 and 57 px
   under it; with the scarf the next-round line was above the bar on 4 of 9 states in French and 1 of 9 in Arabic. That is the cost
   of the 296 × 479 px card the plan asked for (§10). [`results/first-screen.txt`](wp4/results/first-screen.txt) (branch) and
   [`results/first-screen-base.txt`](wp4/results/first-screen-base.txt) (`main`).
3. **The tilt does not hold 60 fps here.** A pointer circling the card for 3 s in this headless, software-rendered Chromium: the
   old card on `main` 16.7 ms a frame (60 fps), no long task, at CPU x1 and x4; the new card without the tilt (reduced motion) the
   same; **the new card with the tilt 33 to 50 ms a frame at CPU x1 with 2 to 7 tasks over 50 ms, 67 to 83 ms and 18 to 31 such tasks
   at x4**; a page with no card 16.7 ms. The cost is the tilt itself. Whether a phone or laptop GPU keeps 60 fps is **not shown**.
   [`results/tilt.txt`](wp4/results/tilt.txt), [`tilt-base.txt`](wp4/results/tilt-base.txt), [`tilt-reduced.txt`](wp4/results/tilt-reduced.txt).
4. **The number against its shirt, with the pointer over it, reads a hair under 3:1** on 6 of 40 cards at 296 px and 8 of 24 at
   336 px (2.94 to 2.98): the forming card's dash and LEGEND. At rest every card is 3.00 or more. The probe's histogram method on the
   same boxes gives 3.51 to 3.93. See criterion V3.
5. **The outer edge on a light page.** CHAMPION and LEGEND's outermost line reads 2.83 to 2.98 against white (3 of 40 cards at
   296 px, 4 of 20 at 336 px, floor 3); in the dark theme, with the seven thickness walls counted, the silhouette's edge reads 1.96 to
   2.21 on the trailing side (the walls are near black on a near-black page). See criterion V3.
6. **One decorative separator, 10 times, in Arabic, light theme.** The repository's contrast probe reads the « · » between the
   rating and the tier (`p[data-testid=gradins-rating-line] > span`, `aria-hidden`) at 2.2 to 2.28:1 against its 3:1 floor on 10 of
   the 24 Arabic screens; `main` reads 0 on the three routes tried there (`/gradins?mc=forming1`, `/gradins?mc=homa`, `/gradins/carte?mc=rated`). The dot is a 5 × 37 px box around a 4 px glyph: direct pixels give 8.27:1
   on the branch and 6.92:1 on `main`, and its CSS colour is 7.43:1 against white, so this is the probe's 0.4 % ink threshold on a
   box that is almost empty, not the dot's colour. Not changed. [`results/contrast-page-light.txt`](wp4/results/contrast-page-light.txt), [`contrast-page-base-ar.txt`](wp4/results/contrast-page-base-ar.txt), [`separator-dot.txt`](wp4/results/separator-dot.txt).
7. **« Ma saison, rang par rang »** (`gradins.share.caption`) still names the knitting in the share picture's heading. It is copy,
   not art; plan §12.3 only covered code and test names.
8. **The Fantasy team page's pitch runs its `enter-rise` entrance under reduced motion** (5 finished animations, French and
   Arabic). It is the app's own, in `src/styles.css`, unchanged by this branch, and not the card (0 cards on that page: the born
   panel draws a token). No Gradins screen has any (0 of 32).
9. **Fonts.** The Impeccable detector lists « Instrument Serif » (the plan's owner question 2, default yes) as a font DESIGN.md
   does not declare. DESIGN.md was not edited here.
