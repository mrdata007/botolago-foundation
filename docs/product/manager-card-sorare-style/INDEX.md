# Manager Card collectible redesign: evidence index

Every acceptance criterion of the [brief](../MANAGER_CARD_SORARE_STYLE_BRIEF.md), the command that measured it, the
port, the number, and the file that holds it. The design is the [plan](../MANAGER_CARD_SORARE_STYLE_PLAN.md) (revision 3
sections win over older text) and the preview [`mock.html`](mock.html). The card is the collectible (Éclat, renderer
`eclat-v1`); the incumbent it replaces is the Écharpe on `main` at `8fae526c`.

Everything below was measured on `claude/manager-card-sorare-style` after round 2 and its review fixes (plan §18: the last source commit is **`18f921ee`**,
the review fixes; the integration before it was `98a02df1`; round 1 was at `5e903f60`, after the review fixes of §17), in `/home/user/mc-sorare`, with `src/`, `public/`, `scripts/` and `tests/` exactly as committed.
Nothing here touched a database, a migration, an Edge Function, a deployment or Lovable: every run used the mock data
modes (`VITE_*_DATA_MODE=mock`, `VITE_AUTH_MODE=mock`), or the production build against the e2e stub backend. Nothing was
pushed. One writer at a time: the only servers of this evidence were the ones named below, each started and stopped by its process id (this run: 4490, for the development server of this tree, then, one at a time, for the production builds of this tree and of `origin/main`, and for a development server of `origin/main`; the integration of round 2 used 4440 and 4441); other sessions' work (a Prettier run, for one) shared the machine now and then.

Measured as CLAUDE.md "Evidence" asks: overflow from element rectangles (never `scrollWidth`), contrast from rasterised
pixels (never from the colours in the markup), each run against a server started from the tree being measured on its own
port. Where a check did not give what the brief wants, the row says so, in numbers.

## What did not run, and why

- **A production figure for G1's time to card.** `MANAGER_CARD_PREVIEW` needs `import.meta.env.DEV`, so the section can only be
  drawn on a development server (unbundled modules, the development build of React). The figures below are upper bounds.
- **The tilt on a GPU.** The only browser here is headless Chromium with a software rasteriser. See finding 3.
- **The touch float on a real touch screen.** Chromium's touch emulation (`isMobile`, `hasTouch`, `(hover: none)`) is all that measured it (finding 11).
- **The Impeccable detector in the finishing session.** It reads `.tsx` and `.css` files and none changed since the integration's run (the review fixes touch
  `.ts` files, their tests and the README), so F9's result is the integration's.
- **Anything against a real backend or a real account.** Mock modes and fixtures only; no database access of any kind.
- **A real phone, Safari, a screen reader.** Not available. Accessible names are asserted by the render tests; contrast, overflow and
  touch sizes are measured in Chromium at phone widths.
- **A draft pull request.** This package may not push, so none was opened. [`PR_DESCRIPTION.md`](PR_DESCRIPTION.md) is the
  description, ready to paste, with the brief in it. Not merged, not published, nothing deployed.
- **The Impeccable detector on the `.ts` files** that draw the card's SVG: it reads `.tsx` and `.css` only (see criterion F9).
- **The after pictures with the section off.** With the section off the app is today's app; that is shown by the switch-off
  comparison (pictures of the production builds), not by another picture set.

## Findings that need the owner's eye

Round 1 of this evidence measured these and changed none of them (this package measures, it does not edit the app). Round 2 (the
integration of 2026-10-10: three lanes, the sheen mask, DESIGN.md, the design preview's grids) acted on 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 9; the
status is in each item, and the numbers are from the integration tree's own runs (4440, `wp4/run-measurements.sh`).
The review of the integrated head (items 10 to 13) was fixed in the finishing commit `18f921ee`, and every number in this index was taken again on that tree
(4490); where an item still quotes the integration's figure, it says so.

1. **G1 in French, 768 px and wider: « Les vôtres » was cut to « Les … ». Fixed in round 2.** The card's column is 352 px, so the
   people block's column beside it is 248 px: the heading (103 px) and « Voir les cartes de la ligue » (173 px) with the 12 px gap
   need 288. `PeopleBlock` now has its own header (the same display face and 44 px link as `SectionHeader`, `flex-wrap`, the heading
   without `truncate`): in French from 768 px the link goes under the heading instead of cutting it; in Arabic and on phones it stays
   on one line. Measured with `heading.scrollWidth > clientWidth` at 768, 1024, 1280 and 1440: **0 truncated headings in French and
   in Arabic at all four** (before: « Les vôtres » cut at all four in French, box 63 px, text 103 px), [`results/heading-truncation.txt`](wp4/results/heading-truncation.txt);
   and by a Range over the heading's contents against its box (the e2e `cutHeadings`, which `scrollWidth` alone would miss), every
   heading of 13 Gradins screens at 320, 390, 768, 1024, 1280, 1440 and 1920 in both languages: 882 headings, 0 cut (layout lane). The
   card is still 336 px; nothing was shrunk.
2. **G1's first screen on a phone. Fixed down to a 740 px screen in round 2; below about 730 px it cannot be.** The owner stage's
   card now follows the phone's height (232 to 296 px, plan D16 round 2): at 390 × 844 the next-round line ends **19.89 px (French)
   and 26.98 px (Arabic) above the bottom bar for every card state** (round 1: 7.55 and 2.61), at 360 × 740 **7.09 and 8.16 px**
   (round 1: 96 and 101 px _under_ the bar). It clears the bar by 16 px or more from a 750 px height up. Below that the card is at its
   232 px floor and the line ends under the bar: 375 × 667 by 66 px, 360 × 640 by 93 px, 320 × 568 by 165 to 173 px; no card smaller
   than 232 px was drawn, because the number, the tier and the stats stop being legible. `main` (the Écharpe card) at the same
   sizes: 390 × 844, the line above the bar on 9 of 9 states in French and 6 of 9 in Arabic (homa, arabicName and longNameLatin
   under it); 360 × 740, 4 of 9 and 1 of 9. This branch: 9 of 9 in both languages at both sizes. [`results/first-screen.txt`](wp4/results/first-screen.txt) (branch),
   [`first-screen-base.txt`](wp4/results/first-screen-base.txt) (`main`), [`first-screen-sizes.txt`](wp4/results/first-screen-sizes.txt)
   (14 phone sizes; `scrollWidth` equals the viewport at every one). On a first visit in a session the first-visit hero fills the
   first screen and the rating, identity and next-round lines are under the bar until it is closed: by design (the scripts and the
   e2e close it first, as `sessionStorage` `botolago.card.hero_session.v1` does).
3. **The tilt did not hold 60 fps. Much improved in round 2; still not clean.** Round 1: a pointer circling the card for 3 s in this
   headless, software-rendered Chromium made the new card 33 to 50 ms a frame at CPU x1 with 2 to 7 tasks over 50 ms, 67 to 83 ms and
   18 to 31 such tasks at x4, where the old card on `main` and the new card with the tilt off ran at 16.7 ms. Cause: the tilt wrote two
   inherited custom properties on the card's root, so every SVG element was restyled and every layer repainted on each frame. Round 2
   (perf lane, `eclat/tilt.ts`, `pose.ts`, `lift.ts`, README « Tilt performance ») moves layers instead of repainting them: a frame is
   compositing only. Measured again on the finished tree (`wp4/motion.mjs --only=tilt`, same 3 s circle, four runs in one session; `results/tilt.txt` is the first, `results/tilt-repeat.txt`
   has them all): **CPU x1: median frame 16.7 ms, p95 16.8 to 33.3 ms; rated 0 to 1 long task (56 to 61 ms) and 0 to 15 frames over 20 ms, LEGEND 1 long task
   (62 to 79 ms) and 5 to 15 frames over 20 ms. CPU x4: rated median 16.7 ms, 1 to 4 long tasks (50 to 66 ms), 27 to 34 of 133 to 149 frames over 20 ms; LEGEND median
   33.3 ms in two runs and 16.7 ms in two, 2 to 4 long tasks (52 to 73 ms), 49 to 59 of 93 to 120 frames over 20 ms.** The integration's own figure for LEGEND at x4 (median 16.7 ms,
   46 of 126 frames over 20 ms) was not reproduced; with the three edge files of `777bf6db` put back, three runs in the same session gave 59 to 62 of 91 to 106
   frames over 20 ms and a median of 33.3 ms, so the difference is the machine (load average 1.6 to 2.9, other sessions' work on it) and not the review fixes. A page with no
   card: 16.7 ms and no long task. The sheen mask of round 2 costs nothing measurable (the same runs with it taken out: x1 0 to 1 long task, x4 2 to 3, inside the
   spread of the runs with it). The perf lane measured more, on a busier machine, with its own scripts (scratch, not committed): mean frame
   rate over a 3 s sweep 57 to 59 fps at 296 px and 47 to 58 fps at 336 px (the software rasteriser's draw time grows with layer area);
   at 336 px one long task of 59 to 100 ms remains at each pointer entry (the commit waits for the compositor to raster the newly
   promoted 3D layers), which GPU raster would not have; pixel difference against the old tilt over 224 capture pairs: median 0.009 % of
   pixels. Whether a phone or laptop GPU keeps 60 fps is **still not shown**. [`results/tilt.txt`](wp4/results/tilt.txt) (round 2), [`tilt-base.txt`](wp4/results/tilt-base.txt) and [`tilt-reduced.txt`](wp4/results/tilt-reduced.txt) (the old card and the new one with the tilt off, round 1, not repeated).

    Two causes, each isolated on the running card: on CHAMPION and LEGEND the foil's edge band lay over the 3-unit line and washed it out (1.6 to 2.4 against white), and on every
    tier the tilt resamples the layer while the line, half of which lies outside the layer's box and is clipped, showed 0.44 px at 296 px, under one device pixel. The line is now

    readings under 3: the base card, STADE and CHAMPION on the dark page, the three tiers whose walls round 2 darkened, finding 5)**, because the walls are near black on a near-black page; the first 2 CSS px as a band read 2.53 (19 of 200 under 3
    at 296 px, 7 of 120 at 336 px).
11. **On a phone the float lost its depth half a second after it started. Fixed (a blocker in the review).** On a touch-only screen the card eases to the float's first pose over
    450 ms; the end of that turn, a finger's `pointerleave` after a tap and the `pointercancel` of a pan were all taken for the end of a settle and flattened the card (the layers'
    heights and the number's crop), which no unit test fired. The reviewer's probe in a `hasTouch` and `isMobile` context at 390 × 844 read depth at 100 ms, easing back at 300 ms and `none`
    on every leaf from about 700 ms. Now a touch `pointerleave` and `pointercancel` are ignored, the float's own opening turn is not a settle, and a pen that has left a floating card
    puts the float back (`eclat/tilt.ts`). The new e2e (`on a touch-only phone`) reads `none` on the old file and `matrix3d` on the frame's leaf and on the number, with the crop kept,
    1.5 s after the card appears and 1 s after a tap, on the fix; three new unit cases in `tilt.test.ts` fail on the old file too. [`results/e2e-gradins.txt`](wp4/results/e2e-gradins.txt).
12. **The float started over a beat. Fixed.** Plan §8.3 and §9 say the float pauses while a beat plays; round 2 had removed the CSS rule that did it and its test only read a string
    from `lift.ts`. The tilt now wants the float only when the card's root has no `data-mc-beat` and starts it on the drawing that follows the beat; `tilt.test.ts` mounts the tilt on a
    root with the attribute and checks that nothing floats, runs or is written (it fails on the old file).
13. **The e2e's « flat again » check did not look at what the tilt moves. Fixed.** Since round 2 the frame's depth is written on `.mc-leaf--frame`, the rims' on `.mc-rims`, and the cast
    shadow and the lifted pieces are `.mc-cast` and `.mc-live`; the check read the inner `svg` only. `depth()` now reads all of them and `will-change` on every element, and the rest,
    after-leaving and reduced-motion blocks assert no 3D transform and no `will-change`. With a `translateZ` and a `will-change` planted on the frame's leaf, the rims and the cast,
    the new query sees 3 transforms and 3 `will-change`, the old one 0.
