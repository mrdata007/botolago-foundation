## Questions for the Lead

1. **Line-ups link cost.** The map comes from the Fantasy pool (about 6 pages of 100 plus the stats read), shared with the Fantasy lists and held 10 minutes, fetched only when line-ups are on screen. A one-call RPC (football player id to Fantasy player id for the season) would be cheaper at a derby peak. Accept the pool read for now, or add the RPC in the server branch?
2. **Keyboard access to the pitch.** The pitch is hidden from assistive technology, so its links are touch-only (`tabindex=-1`); the screen-reader XI list is plain text because an invisible focus stop fails the visible-focus rule. Bench and list-form rows are real links. Fine, or should the screen-reader list carry links that show on focus?
3. **Arabic titles** (my wording, e.g. "مباريات البطولة الاحترافية — النتائج المباشرة | BotolaGO") and the new Arabic strings (`fpl.join_code_hint`, `club.live_match`, `fantasy.team.screen_title`) need the owner's Arabic review.
4. **M5 for Arabic body text:** I applied the whole §1.1 table (body 16, secondary 15, meta 14, label/micro 13). Visible consequences: taller Arabic rows on the Fantasy players list at 320 (stats wrap to 3 lines), standings chips wrap to two lines at 320. If that is too much, drop body/secondary/meta and keep only label/micro.
5. **`--ui-control-edge`** has no consumer: field borders use the 14 % `--ui-rule`, which is below 3:1. Switching fields to the new edge colour would darken every form. Do it in Batch 7, or now?
6. **Remaining sub-12px text** is the crest monogram, rank arrow and "م" unit; the spec says floor 12px. Raise them here, or leave for Batch 7?
7. **`FantasyOnboarding.tsx`** (unimported): delete with its keys and adjust `LandingPage.test.tsx` and the W3 baseline, or keep?
8. **Pre-existing, not caused here:** at 320px in Arabic the rankings table squeezes manager names to one or two letters ("D r.") before and after this change.
