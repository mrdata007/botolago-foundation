# BotolaGO UX writing glossary

French addresses the reader as **vous**. Arabic uses natural Modern Standard
Arabic, direct singular address and Western digits. Adapt sentences to the
language; do not reproduce French syntax mechanically. Shared messages written
by the reader to friends may use a familiar register.

## Amounts and factual claims

- The Fantasy budget and player prices use the existing **million-based game
  scale**. Explain “millions” at first use; compact M / م is appropriate in dense
  price displays with an accessible full label. Do not invent a real currency,
  rename the scale to credits, or alter numeric values.
- Keep Fantasy money separate from **points deducted** and from BotolaGO+
  payments in **MAD**. Prices, season end dates and game rules come from their
  existing authoritative sources.
- Distinguish a player's current price, the purchase price and the server's
  selling price. “Budget disponible” for a replacement includes that sale value;
  “Budget restant” is the unspent balance.
- Use “matchs” consistently in French. Keep BotolaGO, BotolaGO+ logos and LASTREET
  in their approved forms. Arabic offer naming intentionally differs from French.
- Use the existing plural helpers and isolated number/name/code runs. Avoid
  attaching a fixed Arabic plural to every possible count.
- Describe the available action and its outcome. Do not promise publication or
  activation times, player participation, delivery, or permanent benefits without
  implementation evidence. Keep provisional and unavailable states explicit.

Existing owner-approved terminology tests remain authoritative for approved
terms. This glossary consolidates them; it does not replace the scoring rules.
